Open https://jasonz0829vip.github.io/fieldnotes-travel-zine/preview/v37/
Real streets, waterways and parks require first-time access to the OpenFreeMap public vector tile service. Subsequent requests to previously viewed areas are cached in browser. If the map service is unavailable, the editor falls back to genuine region outlines; no invented street maps.
Exact GPS is used only when supplied by photo EXIF or explicitly entered. A chosen administrative area without GPS uses its regional center and should not be mistaken for the exact location of a photo.
Photos remain on the device. Approximate map-view coordinates may be sent to OpenFreeMap to retrieve real cartographic details.
No changes to stable homepage / iOS share / Android WeChat save logic.
Third party cartography: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL) and © OpenMapTiles; see THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt.

Important: V3.7 artistic templates currently differ in map placement, cartographic weight and typography, but are an experimental preview, not a rated aesthetics benchmark. Feedback labels are directional and do not invoke generative AI.
